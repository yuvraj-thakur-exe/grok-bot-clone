# User research plan (not yet conducted)

> **Document baseline:** ORBIT 1.0.0-draft (2026-11-08). Earlier version labels are historical unless explicitly marked current.

No interviews, surveys or usability tests are claimed in this package. Conduct discovery before locking the hosted-service roadmap.

## Questions to validate

- Which real tasks do users want ORBIT to execute end-to-end?
- Which tasks are currently slow or failure-prone across chat, browser, terminal and files?
- What level of browser session persistence is acceptable?
- Which tasks require approval, and how should risk be explained?
- Are users primarily local-first developers, individual researchers, or teams?
- Which model providers and monthly budgets are acceptable?
- What makes a user trust a completed action and its evidence?

## Suggested method

Interview 8–12 target users across developer/researcher/operations personas as an initial qualitative sample. Observe task workflows with synthetic accounts and non-sensitive data. Prototype task timeline and approval interaction before high-risk automation. Run usability sessions with keyboard and assistive technology users. This suggested sample is exploratory, not statistically representative.

## Outputs

Anonymized findings, workflow pain points, ranked jobs-to-be-done, feature assumptions accepted/rejected, usability issues, consent notes and product requirement changes. Do not collect secrets or sensitive personal data for discovery.
