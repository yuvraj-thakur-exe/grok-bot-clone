# ADR-NNN: Decision title

> **Document baseline:** ORBIT 1.0.0-draft (2026-11-08). Earlier version labels are historical unless explicitly marked current.

- Status: Proposed / Accepted / Superseded
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: names/roles
- Related requirements: FR-xxx, NFR-xxx

## Context

What problem or constraint requires a decision?

## Decision

What exactly will be done?

## Alternatives considered

List meaningful options and why they were accepted/rejected.

## Consequences

Benefits, costs, risks, operational impact and migration implications.

## Validation

Tests, measurements, rollout and rollback evidence required.

## ORBIT v0.2 alignment

When a feature or ADR concerns native desktop access, include device identity, active session, local/central policy, lease, OS capability, data classification, retry disposition, approval and platform test evidence.
