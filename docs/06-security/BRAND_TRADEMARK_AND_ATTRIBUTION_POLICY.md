# Brand, trademark and attribution policy

> **Document baseline:** ORBIT 1.0.0-draft (2026-11-08). Earlier version labels are historical unless explicitly marked current.

## ORBIT identity

All ORBIT product surfaces should use original ORBIT-owned names, icons, layouts and design tokens. Third-party names may appear in developer settings, integration details and legal/compliance pages where identifying the selected integration or satisfying attribution is necessary.

## Prohibited

- Reusing vendor logos or proprietary iconography as ORBIT branding.
- Copying screenshots, marketing graphics, illustrations, product UI compositions or distinctive trade dress without permission.
- Removing copyright/license text from redistributed source or binaries.
- Using wording that implies official partnership, endorsement or ownership.
- Replacing upstream product names in source attribution or SBOM with ORBIT labels.

## Permitted naming

Use neutral labels: “Browser automation”, “Desktop automation”, “External agent backend”, “Skills registry”, “Sandbox runtime”, “Local development router”, “Generative UI”. Settings may state “Powered by [third-party component]” where accurate and appropriate; notices and source links remain accessible.

## Release checks

- Search UI bundle, docs, screenshots, icons, generated assets, metadata and README for accidental third-party branding.
- Verify ORBIT logo/assets have documented origin and compatible licenses.
- Verify third-party notices remain in `About`, `Open source licenses`, or another accessible compliance surface and in distributed artifacts as required.
- Trademark clearance for ORBIT name is a release gate for public commercial use.
