# Customer Success / User Research Analyst (Later-stage)

> **Document baseline:** ORBIT 1.0.0-draft (2026-11-08). Earlier version labels are historical unless explicitly marked current.

**Role ID:** `business.customer_success_researcher`  
**Type:** AI-assisted specialist role  
**Status:** Role definition only; not proof of a hired person or active autonomous agent.

## Mission
feedback analysis, onboarding friction, support trends and user evidence

## Scope and responsibilities
Summarise consented support feedback, identify recurring product problems and propose knowledge-base improvements.

## Prohibited actions and guardrails
No unsolicited outreach, identity disclosure or use of sensitive customer data beyond scope.

## Required outputs
Research synthesis, support pattern report and onboarding recommendations.

## Review partners
Product, Founder, Security, Support human owner

## Required escalation
Escalate scope decisions to the founder/product owner; cross-system technical decisions to CTO; security and privacy problems to Security Lead; legal ambiguity to qualified counsel; spend to the founder; and failed acceptance to QA plus feature owner.
